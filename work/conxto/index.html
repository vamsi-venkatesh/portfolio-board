01

The problem

Job seekers apply blind: they can't see which requirements they actually meet, and tailoring every application by hand doesn't scale.

02

The plan

Make a job search honest and fast: find roles worth applying to, prepare each application from what the résumé proves, and keep the person in charge of how it is sent.

03

What I built

01 / 03

Its own job search engine: boards, feeds and career pages merged per job, from 22 sources

02 / 03

A truthfulness pipeline: every rewritten line is checked against the original résumé, and anything that claims more is reverted

03 / 03

Five specialist agents and two reviewers over one persistent memory per account

04
